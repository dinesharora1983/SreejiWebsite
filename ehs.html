The operating problem EHS solves

Safety observations often get captured late, summarized manually, assigned informally, and reviewed after the risk has already aged.

  • Reporters need a simple way to capture events with images and structured context.
  • Safety managers need live risk, status, overdue, and closure views.
  • Action owners need a focused workbench for assigned corrective actions.

What AI adds to the workflow

AI enablement strengthens safety operations by helping classify events, prioritize action, summarize narratives, and support corrective-action drafting.

  • Image-first reporting can support richer incident context.
  • AI-generated suggestions can reduce form-entry friction and improve consistency.
  • Management summaries can turn raw event data into review-ready insights.

Features With Screenshots

Each EHS capability is explained with the corresponding working screen.

Operational dashboards

Live safety visibility across open, closed, overdue, and risk-trend data.

The EHS dashboard gives safety leaders a single workspace for total events, open actions, closed actions, overdue closures, SLA breaches, due-soon work, and assigned items.

  • Spot overdue closures and SLA breaches without waiting for manual reports.
  • Review monthly event movement, risk distribution, status mix, site-type mix, and event-type trends.
  • Support leadership reviews with live operational indicators.
Event registry

Searchable incident and observation register.

The event registry centralizes active and closed safety events with filters for status, risk, site type, state, zone, event type, APO, and date range.

  • Find the right complaint or observation faster.
  • Separate active work from closed history while preserving context.
  • Move directly from registry controls into reporting a new event.
AI-assisted event reporting

Image-first reporting for incidents and unsafe conditions.

The report-event workflow starts with incident images, then continues into structured event details. If no images are available, the reporter can explicitly mark that and proceed.

  • Capture visual evidence before the narrative is written.
  • Use AI-generated suggestions where images and details support classification.
  • Improve consistency in event capture for UCS and O&M site workflows.
Reports and exports

Focused report workspaces for review and MIS outputs.

EHS reports group related views such as overview, plant summaries, action tracking, risk and cause, daily operations, and user access.

  • Use report families instead of scrolling through one long data dump.
  • Export event register, action plan, SLA breach, Excel, and PDF outputs.
  • Support monthly submissions, audit meetings, and operating reviews.
APO workbench

Action-owner queue for assigned and overdue work.

The APO workbench gives action owners a focused queue for assigned events, overdue closures, due-soon items, and closed actions with search and filter controls.

  • Keep corrective action work separate from general reporting.
  • Prioritize overdue and high-risk events quickly.
  • Move from assigned work to the full event registry when context is needed.
