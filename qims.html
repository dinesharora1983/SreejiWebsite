The operating problem QIMS solves

Quality inspection workflows often spread across purchase-order lists, supplier emails, inspection documents, revision trails, manual status sheets, and report decks.

  • Supplier, quality, partner, and HOD teams need a shared source of truth.
  • Management needs stage workload, delays, exceptions, and dispatch readiness without manual consolidation.
  • Documents and revisions need traceability so inspection readiness is defensible.

What QIMS gives the business

QIMS turns inspection operations into a governed workflow platform with queues, PO/LOI controls, document revision management, reports, logs, and role-based administration.

  • Faster visibility into where work is blocked.
  • Better supplier coordination from open PO to inspection closure.
  • Audit-ready operational reporting and exportable outputs.

Features With Screenshots

Each QIMS capability is explained with the corresponding working screen.

Operational control center

Live command view for inspection workload and throughput.

The dashboard helps leaders understand inspection health at a glance: active workflows, completion, overdue items, accepted-for-dispatch quantity, throughput trend, and stage concentration.

  • See where inspection work is accumulating before it becomes an escalation.
  • Review current workload by stage and latest workflow activity in one place.
  • Use date-period controls for daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and custom review cycles.
Process queue

One queue for supplier, quality, partner, and HOD work.

The process queue organizes QIMS flows by purchase order, stage, path, and status so each team can find its assigned work and open a dedicated detail page.

  • Search by QIMS reference, PO, supplier, or stage.
  • Separate open and closed purchase-order flows without losing context.
  • Create COC or ICS work directly from the queue when the process requires it.
PO and LOI control

Purchase-order visibility with synchronization support.

The PO/LOI workspace gives teams a structured list of purchase orders, project references, suppliers, item counts, and open status, with a clear synchronization action for upstream systems.

  • Use one searchable purchase-order list instead of scattered PO files.
  • Connect inspection initiation to PO status and supplier identity.
  • Support integration-oriented operations through a dedicated sync action.
Document revisions

Revision control for inspection documents and supporting files.

Document revisions keep quality evidence organized so users can review what changed, who needs to act, and which version belongs to the inspection workflow.

  • Reduce confusion around latest drawings, certificates, and inspection documents.
  • Track revision activity alongside operational workflow status.
  • Improve audit readiness by keeping document changes in the system of record.
Governed reporting

Management reporting with export-ready operational views.

QIMS reports consolidate workflow performance, supplier performance, partner performance, and open-stage distribution with print and CSV export actions.

  • Prepare review meetings without manual spreadsheet consolidation.
  • Compare throughput, stage distribution, and performance indicators in governed report tabs.
  • Export operational evidence for leadership, compliance, or partner review.
