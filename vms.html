Features With Screenshots

Each capability below maps to a working VMS screen.

Command center

Director dashboard for operations, revenue, and governance.

The command center gives leadership a single view of request volume, open work, active quotes, AR, collected revenue, users, and priority actions.

  • Spot pending dispatch, quote update requests, and customer waits.
  • Track workflow health without asking teams for manual status updates.
Case workflow

Request board for every visa and document case.

Teams can review active requests, stages, customer context, aging, and ownership from a structured workboard instead of spreadsheets and chat threads.

  • Reduce missed follow-ups and unclear handoffs.
  • Keep customer, service, status, and action history attached to the case.
Commercial approvals

Quotation workflow with revision visibility.

VMS keeps commercial approvals connected to the service request, allowing quote preparation, customer updates, revision handling, and approval tracking.

  • Shorten the quote-to-approval cycle.
  • Prevent quotation updates from disappearing in email trails.
Billing lifecycle

Invoice tracking from generation to realization.

The invoice workspace helps accounts and management see generated invoices, dispatch status, outstanding receivables, and revenue progress.

  • Convert completed work into revenue faster.
  • Keep AR and invoice actions visible to leadership.
Business KPIs

Reporting for workload, revenue, and governance.

Reporting consolidates the operational signals that matter: open work, stages, revenue, service catalog coverage, role distribution, and recent activity.

  • Review business KPIs without manual report compilation.
  • Use data to manage staffing, follow-up, and governance routines.
